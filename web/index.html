HUMAN-LIKE MEMORY RETRIEVAL

      
Search the way you remember.

      
AFTERIMAGE doesn't force you to remember filenames or exact keyword strings. Its retrieval pipeline extracts recollections using human memory heuristics:

      
    
        
  • Natural Sentences & Fillers — Phrases like “I saw something in the OOPS that says...” are stripped down to core concepts automatically.
    • 
        
  • Temporal Recollection — Mentioning “last week”, “yesterday”, or “last month” prioritizes files modified within that exact time frame.
    • 
        
  • Typo & Irregular Typing — Misspellings like “assignmnet” or “schedulng” are instantly mapped to their true intent.
    • 
        
  • Concept Knowledge Graph — Searching for “OOPS” expands into classes, objects, inheritance, polymorphism.
    • 
        
  • Exact Page References — PDF and multi-page results highlight the exact page where your thoughts match.
    • 
      

      
All indexing and retrieval runs locally on your machine.
